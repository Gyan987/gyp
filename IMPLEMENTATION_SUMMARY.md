## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. Click "Start Game"
3. Use arrow keys or on-screen controls:
   - Left/Right arrows: Change lanes
   - Up arrow: Jump over obstacles
   - Down arrow: Slide under obstacles
4. Collect coins for bonus points
5. Avoid obstacles to survive longer
6. Try to beat your high score!
